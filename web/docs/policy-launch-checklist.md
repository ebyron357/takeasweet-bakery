# Policy launch checklist

These items are genuine launch blockers. Do not infer or publish answers without bakery-owner approval and appropriate legal review.

- [ ] Add the approved privacy contact address to `PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL`.
- [ ] Approve a personal-data retention and deletion schedule.
- [ ] Approve refund, cancellation, rescheduling, and no-show terms.
- [ ] Approve standard and custom-order lead times.
- [ ] Confirm the exact local-delivery boundary, schedule, and fees.
- [ ] Approve ingredients, allergens, and cross-contact language.
- [ ] Approve substitution and handmade-appearance expectations.
- [ ] Confirm sales-tax handling with the payment configuration.
- [ ] Review the privacy notice and ordering information with qualified counsel.
- [ ] Keep payments and submissions gated until the applicable blockers are resolved.

The application intentionally does not publish unapproved answers for these items.
